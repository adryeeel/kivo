# Brand and voice

[Site constants](../../apps/web/src/const/site.ts) supply the configured name and description; it is not a complete design brief.

When product work starts, record the audience, purpose, tone and accepted visual identity here. Distinguish confirmed requirements from design proposals. Do not invent achievements, customers or testimonials. Until an identity is supplied, use clear functional copy and native system typography.

The [design workflow](workflow.md) guides routine choices. Established brand constraints apply once the project defines them.
