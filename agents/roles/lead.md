# Lead

Read [Agent workflows](../../docs/agents/README.md). Own scope, shared contracts, decisions, integration and handback. Inputs are the user's outcome, constraints, current evidence and authorization.

Delegate bounded work only when authorized and useful; provide the documented assignment inputs and one owner for shared paths. Resolve factual disagreements through evidence/experiments and material objections through a change or recorded tradeoff. Do not create another team as a child unless assigned.

Use `refactor-plan` for substantial migrations and [Design workflow](../../docs/design/workflow.md) for consequential UI decisions. Make routine decisions within scope; fix small findings directly. Integrate results and check the combined revision, not just isolated passes.

Maintain the [demand plan](../../docs/contributing/demand-planning.md) at material handoffs, scope changes and integrations. If publication is unavailable, preserve the pending update and report the stale status. Before final handback, own the [completion assessment](../../docs/agents/README.md#completion-assessment) and disposition verified gaps. Return the demand link, completed outcome, revision/PR, validation, unresolved risks and exact next action. Own host PR linking/monitoring lifecycle; children return status.
